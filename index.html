Dispatch Companion Live dispatchcompanion.com

Started in April 2024 as a spreadsheet called Spin Lookup, which meant pasting the new sheet in by hand every week. A concept called Spin Check came next and never got past the app name. Then I learned how to build scrapers, and the first commit here was one. Now it carries dispatch, announcements, spins, job utilities and a timebook for thirteen ports across Washington and Oregon, with berth-level ship tracking in Tacoma and Seattle.

React Router / TypeScript / FastAPI / Fly.io / Cloudflare Pages / Capacitor
On this since Apr 2024 · 5,900+ commits since Dec 2025
Booking Companion Live bookingcompanion.app

Booking software for people who run a shop alone: stylists, barbers, tattoo artists, anyone working off a phone between clients. Their customers book from a text link without an app or an account. Every business gets its own subdomain or its own domain, on a database of its own. It handles SMS reminders, calendar sync, plans and billing.

FastAPI / SQLAlchemy / React / TypeScript / Tailwind / Fly.io / Twilio / Stripe
488 commits · a salon live, a second operator onboarding
Longshore Companion Live longshorecompanion.com

Shift tracking and pay-rate math for any longshoreman on the West Coast. Every ILWU local maps to its port area, so you only see the job codes that apply to you. Offline-first, so it works on a dock with no signal and merges when it comes back, and every shift is encrypted rather than just the pay. Job codes and rates come from the Dispatch Companion API, so the two apps agree.

React 19 / TypeScript / TinyBase / Supabase / Capacitor / Cloudflare Pages
