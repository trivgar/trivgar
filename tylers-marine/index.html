What I fix

Most of it happens on site. If something has to come off the boat and go on a bench, I'll tell you that first.

  1. 01

    Outboards

    Two-stroke and four-stroke, kickers up through the big motors. Service, tune-ups, repairs.

  2. 02

    Inboards and sterndrives

    I/O work. Bellows, gimbal bearings, drive removal, alignment, shift cables.

  3. 03

    It won’t start

    Fuel, spark, compression, wiring. I find out what's actually wrong before I replace anything.

  4. 04

    Winterizing and spring startup

    Fogging, antifreeze, stabilizer, oil and gear lube in the fall. Commissioning in the spring.

  5. 05

    Fuel systems

    Carb rebuilds, injector service, water in the fuel, ethanol gunk, tanks, lines, primer bulbs. Honestly, this is most of what goes wrong.

  6. 06

    Lower units, impellers, props

    Water pump kits, gearcase service, seals, prop swaps. If you hit something, I can tell you how bad it is.

  7. 07

    Electrical

    Batteries, charging systems, trim and tilt, nav lights, switch panels. Also whatever the last owner did to the wiring.

Vessel support

Other things people call me for

A boat is a long list of small jobs that never add up to a haul-out. That's a good part of my week.

  • Dockside and on-water calls, for when it quits on you.
  • Looking a boat over before you buy it. Compression test and a straight answer. It's talked people out of some expensive mistakes.
  • Spring prep and fall layup.
  • Trailer work. Bearings, lights, winches, all the stuff that strands you before you even get wet.
