Services

What I work on

Most jobs get done on site. If it needs to come apart on a bench, I’ll tell you that up front rather than after the invoice.

  1. 01

    Outboard service & repair

    Two-stroke and four-stroke, portable kickers through big blocks. Scheduled maintenance, tune-ups and repairs.

  2. 02

    Inboard & sterndrive

    I/O service, bellows and boot replacement, gimbal bearings, drive removal, alignment and shift cable work.

  3. 03

    No-starts & diagnostics

    Fuel, spark, compression, electrical. I find the actual cause before anything gets replaced.

  4. 04

    Winterizing & spring service

    Fogging, antifreeze, stabilizer, oil and gear lube. Then commissioning in the spring so the first trip isn’t the shakedown.

  5. 05

    Fuel systems

    Carb rebuilds, injector service, ethanol and varnish cleanup, water-in-fuel, tanks, lines and primer bulbs.

  6. 06

    Lower units, impellers & props

    Water pump kits, gearcase service, seal replacement, prop swaps and skeg damage assessment.

  7. 07

    Electrical & charging

    Batteries, charging systems, trim and tilt, switch panels, nav lights and the wiring the last guy left behind.

Vessel support

More than the motor

Owning a boat is a string of small jobs that never quite justify a haul-out. That is most of what I do.

  • Dockside & on-water calls. Dead in the slip or stuck at the ramp — call and we will sort out what it needs.
  • Pre-purchase look-overs. Before you hand over cash for someone else’s project, get a compression test and an honest opinion.
  • Season prep & layup. Getting it ready in the spring, putting it away right in the fall.
  • Trailer & launch help. Bearings, lights, winches and the things that strand you before you reach the water.
