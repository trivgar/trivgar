1986 Porsche 944 Turbo

My 1986 Porsche 944 Turbo. The 944 was Porsche's front-engine sports car in the 80s, and mine's the Turbo. I bought it in January 2007 with 128k miles on it, third owner. I do all my own work on it, same as all my cars. It's running a bigger Garrett turbo now and a VEMS standalone engine computer that I tuned myself.

Aug 2006

The first one. My dad's '83 NA, no turbo. He got it for $500, not running. The day we brought it home.

Sep 2006

Then I bought an '84 NA for $800, Sept 6th. Sold it about a year later.

Feb 2007

The Turbo, a month after I bought it. Third owner, 128k on it.

Sep 2007

Dyno day. 250 horsepower at the wheels on the stock turbo, with just a boost controller, chips and an exhaust. Porsche rated it 217 at the crank from the factory.

Oct 2008

Putting in the bigger turbo, a Garrett-based Lindsey Super 65, with 72 lb injectors and a new airflow sensor to feed it.

Dec 2008

Two Turbos in the garage. My dad got his in August '08. His is on the left, mine's on the right.

Around 2010

By now it had Koni shocks and much stiffer springs, from May 2009.

Nov 2010

Snow day.

Jan 2011

Bought a parts 951 and stripped it completely. It supplied parts to both my dad's guards red '86 951 and mine.

Mar 2011

My '88 NA. It had a slipped timing belt and bent valves. Fixed and sold.

Sep 2011

Engine out, with another one on the stand behind it.

2012

Swapped the block.

Aug 2016

Fresh paint. It was painted that July.

Feb 2020

Me and my dad working on his 951.

Jul 2020

Spec Stage 3 clutch and billet flywheel, a high torque starter, belts and a tune up. The old parts on the floor.

2025

Wizard Cooling aluminum radiator next to the old one, new belts, rollers and seals, and Clewett trigger wheels so VEMS can read the crank and cam.

Mar 2026

Tuning from the passenger seat. VEMS went in back in 2021, and I built the fuel and timing maps myself.

Aug 2026

Primary trigger signal on the oscilloscope, the crank wheel's teeth as VEMS sees them. Cam sync is done now.

What's on it

  • Garrett turbo (Lindsey Super 65)
  • VEMS standalone with wasted spark, tuned myself
  • Siemens 72 lb injectors
  • Konis all around, 968 M030 up front
  • Spec Stage 3 clutch and billet flywheel
  • 993 Cup 2 wheels
