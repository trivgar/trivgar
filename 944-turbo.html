1986 Porsche 944 Turbo

My 1986 Porsche 944 Turbo. The 944 was Porsche's front-engine sports car in the 80s, and mine's the Turbo. I bought it in January 2007 with 128k miles on it, third owner. I do all my own work on it, same as all my cars. It's running a bigger Garrett turbo now and a VEMS standalone engine computer that I tuned myself.

What's on it

  • Garrett turbo (Lindsey Super 65)
  • VEMS standalone with wasted spark, tuned myself
  • Siemens 72 lb injectors
  • Konis all around, 968 M030 up front
  • Spec Stage 3 clutch and billet flywheel
  • 993 Cup 2 wheels

Written by Travis Pellegrini in Tacoma.
